> Legal status in one line: the **WoFlV is a binding federal ordinance** (directly binding for publicly subsidised housing, and the default for rental contracts per BGH case law). **DIN 1356-1, DIN 18011, DIN 18022, DIN 18040-2 are private-law standards = recommendations / generally accepted rules of technology**, unless a Land building code (LBO) or the Technische Baubestimmungen introduce them (DIN 18040-2 is commonly referenced for barrier-free dwellings). **Minimum ceiling heights and parapet/fall-protection heights come from the 16 Landesbauordnungen (binding, vary by Land).**

## 1. How German floor plans are drawn and dimensioned (DIN 1356-1, symbols, dimension chains, notations, scales)

### Takeaway
German plans follow DIN 1356-1 (current edition 2024-04): dimensions under 1 m are written in cm, over 1 m in m, without units, with a superscript digit for half-centimetres/millimetres; openings are labelled as a fraction **width over height** (e.g. 88,5/2,01 or 1,01/2,13⁵ = rough-opening width 1,01 m, height 2,135 m) and BRH = Brüstungshöhe (sill/parapet height); building plans normally show **rough-shell dimensions (Rohbaumaße, without plaster/screed)**, while exposé plans usually show finished dimensions — the plan legend decides.

### Cited Findings
**Standard and status**
- DIN 1356-1 "Bauzeichnungen – Teil 1: Grundregeln der Darstellung" is listed by DIN as current in the edition **2024-04**; a revision draft **E DIN 1356-1:2026-09** exists. — [DIN catalogue DIN 1356-1:2024-04](https://din.de/en/wdc-beuth:din21:325728444); [DIN draft E DIN 1356-1:2026-09](https://www.din.de/en/getting-involved/standards-committees/nabau/drafts/wdc-beuth:din21:403956420). One consumer guide claims DIN 1356-1 was withdrawn before Jan 2025, which contradicts the DIN catalogue entry; treat the DIN entry as authoritative. — [DIN table of contents/download](https://www.din.de/de/mitwirken/normenausschuesse/nabau/veroeffentlichungen/wdc-beuth:din21:325728444/toc-3177416/download)
- The standard's table of contents covers projection types, sheet sizes, **scales (Maßstäbe)**, line types and line widths, **dimensioning (Bemaßung)** and marking of section lines in the plan. — [DIN 1356-1 ToC](https://www.din.de/de/mitwirken/normenausschuesse/nabau/veroeffentlichungen/wdc-beuth:din21:325728444/toc-3177416/download)
- Building drawings must be dimensioned so that all important dimensions (individual and overall) can be read without difficulty; teaching material names DIN 1356 and DIN 406 as the basis. — [Uni Hamburg, "Bauzeichnen I" (PDF)](https://epub.sub.uni-hamburg.de/epub/volltexte/2010/4886/pdf/Bauzeichnen_I.pdf)
- Other references for dimensioning named in guides: DIN 1356 and DIN ISO 7519. — [sanier.de, Baupläne verstehen](https://www.sanier.de/bauen/bauantrag/bauplaene-verstehen-grundrisse-schnitte-und-ansichten)

**Units and number notation**
- Dimensions **under 1 m are given in centimetres without unit symbol** (e.g. "95" = 95 cm), dimensions **over 1 m in metres** (e.g. "3,76"); attributed to DIN 1356-1. A **superscript digit** adds half-centimetres/millimetres: 78,5 cm is written 78⁵; 1,205 m is written 1,20⁵. — [bau.de forum: "Was bedeutet hochgestellte Zahl? (86 hoch 5)"](https://bau.de/forum/architektur/10226.php); [Uni Hamburg, Bauzeichnen I](https://epub.sub.uni-hamburg.de/epub/volltexte/2010/4886/pdf/Bauzeichnen_I.pdf)

**Dimension chains (Maßketten) and reference edges**
- Dimension chains run around the outside of the plan and become more detailed from outside to inside: **outermost = overall dimension, next = wall sections/projections, innermost = individual openings** (windows, doors). — [wunderloop.de, Grundriss lesen](https://wunderloop.de/wissen/bibliothek/grundrisse/grundriss-lesen); [planprofi24.de, Grundriss richtig lesen](https://planprofi24.de/ratgeber/grundriss-richtig-lesen) (search extract; exact attribution between these two guide pages not verifiable)
- Dimensions usually refer to **rough-shell edges (Rohbaukanten), i.e. without later plaster**; plaster edges are used as reference only in exceptions such as renovations. — [plan7architekt, Grundriss richtig bemaßen](https://plan7architekt.com/i/grundriss-richtig-bemassen-ai59/)
- Building plans normally show **Rohbaumaße (without plaster and floor finish)** → a finished room is **a few centimetres smaller**; **as-built plans (Bestandspläne) and exposé plans often show finished dimensions (Fertigmaße)**. — [wunderloop.de](https://wunderloop.de/wissen/bibliothek/grundrisse/grundriss-lesen); [planprofi24.de](https://planprofi24.de/ratgeber/grundriss-richtig-lesen); [sanierungspartner-nord.de, Grundrisse lesen](https://sanierungspartner-nord.de/ratgeber-grundrisse-lesen)
- Rohbaumaße refer to the unfinished building shell; Fertigmaße are final dimensions after plaster, screed (Estrich) and floor coverings. Which kind of dimension a plan uses must be stated on the plan (legend/title block) — there is no automatic rule. — [bau.de forum: Rohbaumaße vs. Fertigmaße](https://bau.de/forum/planung/12927.php); [bau.de forum: Fenstermaß 100/130 – Rohbaulichte, Rahmenaußenmaß, Architekturlichte](https://bau.de/forum/architektur/10789.php)

**Opening notation (doors/windows), BRH**
- Doors carry a fraction such as **88,5 / 2,01: number above the line = rough-opening width (88,5 cm), below = height (2,01 m)**; the clear passage width (lichte Durchgangsbreite) of the finished door is smaller. — [wunderloop.de](https://wunderloop.de/wissen/bibliothek/grundrisse/grundriss-lesen); [planprofi24.de](https://planprofi24.de/ratgeber/grundriss-richtig-lesen)
- A window label like "100/130" can mean rough opening (Rohbaulichte), frame outer dimension or "Architekturlichte" depending on office conventions — must be clarified via legend. — [bau.de forum 10789](https://bau.de/forum/architektur/10789.php)
- **BRH = Brüstungshöhe = distance from floor to the underside of the window (sill)**, e.g. "BRH 90" = 90 cm. — [sanierungspartner-nord.de](https://sanierungspartner-nord.de/ratgeber-grundrisse-lesen); [hausbau-grundrisse.de, Grundriss-Symbole A–Z](https://www.hausbau-grundrisse.de/planungswissen/grundriss-symbole); other abbreviations in plan legends: LH = lichte Höhe — [immogenio guide (PDF)](https://immogenio-landingpage.pages.dev/blog/posts/technik/bauzeichnungen-aufteilungsplan-lesen-grundrisse-schnitte-ansichten.pdf)

**Door opening sizes (DIN 18100 / DIN 18101: Baurichtmaß vs. Nennmaß)**
- **Nennmaß (rough opening) = Baurichtmaß + joint: width +10 mm, height +5 mm**; DIN 18100 "Türen; Wandöffnungen für Türen" governs the wall opening. — [letwork.de, Rohbaumaße Türen & Fenster](https://letwork.de/ratgeber/tueren/normen-und-masse/rohbaumasse-tueren-und-fenster/); [steel-interior.de, Türmaße nach DIN](https://www.steel-interior.de/tuermasse-nach-din/); [lokaleprofis.de, Rohbaumaße Türen](https://lokaleprofis.de/blog/rohbaumase-fur-turen/)
- Manufacturer example: **Baurichtmaß 1000 × 2125 mm → Rohbaumaß/Nennmaß 1010 × 2130 mm**. — [heinze.de / Novoferm data sheet (PDF)](https://heinze.de/m2/40/60840/pdf/85/22238885px595x842.pdf)
- Correspondingly **885 mm width = Baurichtmaß 875 mm + 10 mm** (the common German interior door). — [heimwerker.de, Türmaße – Rohbaumaße](https://www.heimwerker.de/tuermasse-die-richtigen-rohbaumasse-fuer-tueren/)
- In masonry the +10 mm rule applies (2 × 5 mm joints); in drywall the nominal dimension can equal the Baurichtmaß. — [bauexpertenforum, Dry-fix Zarge Baurichtmaß](https://www.bauexpertenforum.de/threads/dry-fix-zarge-baurichtmass-zu-klein.139497/); for jointless constructions Rohbaumaß = Baurichtmaß — [buffl.co, Bauricht- vs. Baunennmaß](https://buffl.co/community/c/65b52e8cf2f8abd87db49f49)

**Symbols**
- **Hinged door**: drawn as an arc (quarter circle) showing the swing area; the straight line projecting into the room = the door leaf, on the hinge (Anschlag) side. — [immoportal.com, Türanschlag](https://www.immoportal.com/node/8177); [bau.de forum Grundriss zeichnen](https://bau.de/forum/dig/10179.php)
- **Handing (DIN links / DIN rechts)** per **DIN 107** (Germany) and **DIN EN 12519** (Europe), identical for doors and windows: standing in front of the closed door on the side where the hinges are visible — hinges on the right = DIN rechts, on the left = DIN links. — [baunetzwissen, DIN-links und DIN-rechts](https://www.baunetzwissen.de/fenster-und-tueren/fachwissen/konstruktion-funktion/din-links-und-din-rechts-155269); [selbst.de, Türanschlag links oder rechts](https://www.selbst.de/tueranschlag-links-oder-rechts-37.html); sources disagree slightly on the viewing side — [bau.de forum 11801](https://bau.de/forum/fenster-tueren/11801.php)
- **Sliding door**: often an arrow in the wall opening showing the sliding direction; double sliding doors = two opposite arrows; pocket doors (not visible when open) may simply be labelled "Schiebetür". — [immostage.ai, Grundriss-Symbole](https://www.immostage.ai/grundriss/symbole/); [myhomebook.de, Grundriss lesen](https://www.myhomebook.de/projects/grundriss-lesen); [hausbau-grundrisse.de](https://www.hausbau-grundrisse.de/planungswissen/grundriss-symbole)
- **Window**: double line inside the wall, width drawn to scale; a quarter circle or diagonal additionally drawn = floor-to-ceiling (bodentief) window; width and often BRH noted next to it. — [hausbau-grundrisse.de](https://www.hausbau-grundrisse.de/planungswissen/grundriss-symbole); [baufoerderung.de, Grundriss-Symbole](https://baufoerderung.de/glossar/grundriss-lesen-symbole-verstehen-und-richtig-interpretieren/)
- **Stairs**: the **Lauflinie** (walking line) runs in the middle of the treads, starts with a small **circle at the lowest step** and ends with an **arrow at the top step** (always points upward); the cut through the stair is shown by two parallel lines at 45° to the Lauflinie; everything above approx. 1 m (above the horizontal section plane) is drawn dashed. — [myhomebook.de](https://www.myhomebook.de/projects/grundriss-lesen); [hausbauexperte.net, Grundriss lesen](https://www.hausbauexperte.net/grundriss-lesen/); [pocasio.com, Wie liest man einen Grundriss](https://www.pocasio.com/ratgeber/bau-lexikon/wie-liest-man-einen-grundriss-eine-praktische-anleitung/)
- **Radiator (Heizkörper)**: narrow elongated rectangle in front of the wall, usually below the window; some guides draw it as a rectangle with horizontal lines. — [hausbau-grundrisse.de](https://www.hausbau-grundrisse.de/planungswissen/grundriss-symbole); [edrawsoft DE, Grundriss-Symbole](https://www.edrawsoft.com/de/article/floor-plan-symbols-and-meanings.html)
- Symbols are not fully uniform; **the plan's legend is authoritative** for symbols, abbreviations and line types. — [baufoerderung.de](https://baufoerderung.de/glossar/grundriss-lesen-symbole-verstehen-und-richtig-interpretieren/); [hausbau-grundrisse.de](https://www.hausbau-grundrisse.de/planungswissen/grundriss-symbole)

**Scales**
- **Planning-permission drawings (Genehmigungsplanung) usually 1:100; construction/working drawings (Ausführungs-/Werkplanung) 1:50; details 1:10 or larger.** At 1:100 only wall thicknesses are drawn; at 1:50 the individual wall layers (masonry, insulation, plaster). Building is done from 1:50 working drawings (+ details), not from 1:100 permit drawings. — [sanier.de, Baupläne verstehen](https://www.sanier.de/bauen/bauantrag/bauplaene-verstehen-grundrisse-schnitte-und-ansichten); details from 1:20 (e.g. window connections) — [greenox-group.de, Bauzeichnung](https://greenox-group.de/bauzeichnung/)

### Inferences
- **Reading "1,01/2,135" (also written 1,01/2,13⁵)**: width 1,01 m = Nennmaß of the 1000-mm Baurichtmaß door (wide door, e.g. apartment entrance or barrier-free). The height 2,135 m does not match the DIN 18100 nominal heights from finished floor (2,005 / 2,130 m) exactly. Two plausible readings: (a) a 2,125-m Baurichtmaß door (Nennmaß 2,130) with 5 mm extra; or (b) more likely in Rohbau plans, the opening height is measured from the **raw slab (OK Rohdecke)** and includes the floor build-up (e.g. 2,005 m + ~13 cm screed/insulation/finish ≈ 2,135 m). A planner should check whether opening heights refer to OKFF (Oberkante Fertigfußboden) or OKRF (Oberkante Rohfußboden) in the legend. (Inference; no source stated this explicitly.)
- **"0,885 / 2,01"** = standard German interior door opening (Baurichtmaß 875 × 2000, colloquially "86er Tür" after its ~860 mm leaf) — the "86er" naming is background knowledge, unverified here.
- At 1:100, 1 cm on paper = 1 m; at 1:50, 2 cm = 1 m (arithmetic). Real-estate PDFs are often rescaled, so measure against a printed dimension, not the scale label.
- For 3D/furniture planning, subtract plaster: Rohbau dimensions between masonry walls shrink by roughly 1–1,5 cm plaster per wall side (background knowledge, unverified), i.e. about 2–3 cm per room dimension; floor build-up reduces clear height relative to raw slab.

## 2. Living area calculation (Wohnfläche) per WoFlV vs. DIN 277; how m² appear on plans

### Takeaway
The WoFlV (Wohnflächenverordnung, in force since 1 Jan 2004) counts floor areas **fully at ≥ 2 m clear height, half at 1–< 2 m, not at < 1 m**, and balconies/loggias/roof gardens/terraces **usually ¼, at most ½**; DIN 277 instead measures gross floor areas without height or balcony reductions, so the same attic flat can be 52 m² by WoFlV but 74 m² by DIN 277. For rent law, a deviation of **more than 10 %** from the contractually stated area is a defect, even when the area was stated as "ca.".

### Cited Findings
- **§ 4 WoFlV (Anrechnung der Grundflächen):** (1) areas of rooms/room parts with clear height **≥ 2 m: counted fully**; (2) **≥ 1 m and < 2 m: counted half**; (3) unheated conservatories (Wintergärten), swimming pools and similar rooms closed on all sides: half; (4) **balconies, loggias, roof gardens and terraces: as a rule one quarter, at most one half**. Areas **< 1 m clear height: not counted**. — [lxgesetze.de § 4 WoFlV](https://lxgesetze.de/woflv/4); [lexmea.de § 4 WoFlV](https://www.lexmea.de/gesetz/woflv/4); [gesetze-im-internet.de § 4 WoFlV (official; fetch blocked in this session)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/woflv/__4.html); [rechner-portal.de, Wohnfläche berechnen](https://rechner-portal.de/haushalt-wohnen/raeume-flaechen/wohnflaeche-berechnen)
- In practice **¼ is the normal case for balconies, ½ the exception** (e.g. high-quality, south-facing, large). — [greenox-group.de, Balkon Wohnflächenberechnung](https://greenox-group.de/balkon-wohnflaechenberechnung/); [rechner-portal.de](https://rechner-portal.de/haushalt-wohnen/raeume-flaechen/wohnflaeche-berechnen)
- Worked example: 75 m² full-height floor + 12 m² sloped area 1–2 m (→ 6 m²) + 4 m² under 1 m (→ 0) + 8 m² balcony (→ 2 m²) = **83 m² Wohnfläche**. — [reduco.ai, Wohnfläche berechnen beim Hausverkauf](https://reduco.ai/blog/immobilienverkauf/wohnflaeche-berechnen-hausverkauf) (guide example; exact source page among search results not verifiable)
- **WoFlV vs. DIN 277:** DIN 277 measures the floor area (Grundfläche) regardless of room height; balconies/terraces count fully; cellars/ancillary rooms count fully, whereas the WoFlV excludes e.g. heating rooms and laundry rooms (Waschküche); conservatories half under WoFlV vs. without deduction under DIN 277. Example: same attic flat **52 m² (WoFlV) vs. 74 m² (DIN 277)**. — [immobilienscout24.de, DIN 277](https://www.immobilienscout24.de/wissen/verkaufen/din277-instanz-fuer-wohn-und-nutzflaechen.html); [ruv.de, Wohnflächenberechnung](https://www.ruv.de/ratgeber/bauen-wohnen/geld-recht/wohnflaechenberechnung); [sanier.de, Wohnflächenberechnung](https://www.sanier.de/wissen/wohnflaechenberechnung)
- **Legal status:** the WoFlV has been the relevant basis for rental flats since 2004; absent a different agreement, the WoFlV applies in case of doubt. — [ista.com, Wohnflächenverordnung](https://www.ista.com/de/gesetze-und-verordnungen/wohnflaechenverordnung/); [mlp.de, Wohnflächenberechnung](https://mlp.de/finanzen-versicherungen/finanzierung/immobilienfinanzierung/themen/wohnflaechenberechnung/)
- **BGH 10 % rule:** an actual area **more than 10 % below** the contractually stated area is a material defect justifying rent reduction; the BGH fixed this threshold for practicability and legal certainty and recognises no further tolerance; the tenant bears the burden of proof. — [Berliner Mieterverein, BGH](https://www.berliner-mieterverein.de/recht/bgh/bgh0416.htm); [haufe.de, BGH 24.03.2004 VIII ZR 133/03](https://haufe.de/id/entscheidung/bgh-urteil-vom-24032004-viii-zr-13303-HI1243843.html); [bmgev.de, Flächenabweichung als Mangel](https://bmgev.de/mietrecht/urteile/flaechenabweichung-der-wohnung-als-mangel-der-mietsache-1)
- **"ca."** does not make the statement non-binding: in BGH VIII ZR 39/09 an area stated as "ca. 88 qm" was tested against the 10 % rule; the area stated in a rental contract is regularly a **Beschaffenheitsvereinbarung** (agreed property), not a non-binding description. — [rewis.io, BGH VIII ZR 39/09](https://rewis.io/urteile/urteil/wcu-15-12-2009-viii-zr-3909/); [rewis.io, BGH VIII ZR 173/17](https://rewis.io/urteile/urteil/j41-16-01-2019-viii-zr-17317/)

### Inferences
- For a planner: a room area printed on a plan ("Wohnen 24,35 m²") is a **WoFlV-reduced figure** if the plan is a Wohnflächenberechnung; under sloped ceilings, the *usable floor* for furniture is larger than the counted m², but standing room (≥ 2 m) is smaller. Furniture taller than the knee wall (Drempel) must be placed in the ≥ 2 m zone or measured against the section.
- Balcony m² on plans may already be the ¼/½ value or the full floor area — check which (common source of confusion).
- Background knowledge, unverified in this session (official § 2/§ 3 WoFlV text was blocked): areas are measured as clear dimensions between the front edges of wall claddings (i.e. plaster included in deduction); excluded are chimneys/pillars/columns > 1,5 m high with > 0,1 m² footprint, stairs with > 3 risers and their landings, door/window recesses (Tür- und Fensternischen) unless floor-level and ≥ 0,13 m deep; ancillary rooms outside the flat (cellar, laundry, heating room, garages) do not count. These should be verified against the official text before being stated as fact.

## 3. Typical German residential dimensions (ceiling heights, wall thicknesses, doors, sills, radiators)

### Takeaway
Minimum clear height for habitable rooms is set by the Land building codes (typically **2,40 m**, e.g. NRW; Berlin 2,50 m; attics/basements partly 2,20 m over half the area); Gründerzeit Altbau is typically **3,00–3,80 m**, post-war 1950s–80s blocks **2,30–2,45 m**, current new builds **2,50–2,60 m**. Typical walls: masonry exterior **36,5–45 cm**, load-bearing interior **17,5–24 cm (min. 11,5)**, non-load-bearing/drywall **~10–12 cm**. Standard door openings 0,885 and 1,01 m wide (Baurichtmaß 875/1000 mm); sill height around **80–90 cm**; panel radiators commonly **600 mm** high.

### Cited Findings
**Ceiling heights (lichte Raumhöhe)**
- NRW (BauO NRW 2018 § 46): habitable rooms need a clear height of **2,40 m**; in attic and basement storeys **2,20 m over at least half** the net floor area suffices. — [lexmea.de, BauO NRW § 46](https://lexmea.de/de/gesetz/bauo-2018-nrw/46)
- Requirements differ by Land, e.g. **Bayern ≥ 2,40 m, Berlin ≥ 2,50 m**. — [mietpreise.info, Deckenhöhe](https://www.mietpreise.info/site/deckenhoehe); 2,40–2,50 m depending on Land — [bau.de forum 10797](https://bau.de/forum/normen/10797.php)
- **Gründerzeit (1850–1900): 3,20–3,80 m**, Berlin often 3,60 m, southern Germany typically 3,20 m; another guide: Altbau before 1940 often **3,00–3,50 m**. — [jacasa.de, Deckenhöhe](https://www.jacasa.de/ratgeber/lexikon/deckenhoehe); [hausjournal.net, normale Zimmerhöhe](https://www.hausjournal.net/normale-zimmerhoehe)
- **1950s–1980s: often only 2,30–2,45 m**; 2,42 m described as typical for 1960s–80s. — [mietpreise.info](https://www.mietpreise.info/site/deckenhoehe); [hausbau-forum.de, Deckenhöhe](https://www.hausbau-forum.de/category/D/deckenhoehe/)
- **New builds: 2,50–2,60 m usual, upscale 2,70 m+**. — [fertighaus.de, Deckenhöhe bei Neubauten](https://www.fertighaus.de/ratgeber/hausbau/deckenhoehe-bei-neubauten/)
- Switzerland: modern new builds often 2,50–2,60 m, 2,40 m standard in the 1980s. — [neho.ch, Raumhöhe Schweiz](https://neho.ch/de/blog/raumhohe-schweiz)
- A forum summary claims DIN 18040-1 requires ≥ 2,50 m clear height for barrier-free building — weak source (AI-generated forum summary), unverified. — [bau.de forum 10037](https://bau.de/forum/normen/10037.php)

**Wall thicknesses**
- Exterior masonry walls **36,5–45 cm**; interior walls **11,5–24 cm**. — [hausbau-forum.de, Wandstärke](https://www.hausbau-forum.de/category/W/wandstaerke/Seite3/)
- Load-bearing interior walls usually **17,5 cm** (or 24 cm); non-load-bearing about **10 cm** (thinner possible but poor sound insulation); with high-strength materials load-bearing 11,5 cm possible. — [sanier.de, Klassische Wanddicken im Hausbau](https://www.sanier.de/bauen/klassische-wanddicken-im-hausbau); [immoportal.com, Innenwände](https://www.immoportal.com/node/8239)
- New builds: load-bearing interior wall minimum 11,5 cm, ideal 17,5 cm; a structural engineer in a forum calls 17,5 cm the practical minimum. — [bau.de forum Mauerstärke Innenwände](https://bau.de/forum/innenwaende/10213.php); [bauexpertenforum](https://www.bauexpertenforum.de/goto/post?id=944209)
- Rental flat example: interior walls drywall or aerated concrete (Ytong) **10–12 cm**. — [hausbau-forum.de, Innenwände](https://www.hausbau-forum.de/category/I/innenwaende/)
- Timber prefab: load-bearing exterior wall ~20 cm, with ETICS ~40 cm. — [sanier.de](https://www.sanier.de/bauen/klassische-wanddicken-im-hausbau)
- **Wall thickness alone does not tell whether a wall is load-bearing** (11,5-cm walls occur both ways); check structural plans. — [bauexpertenforum, Baujahr 1992 – 11,5 cm](https://www.bauexpertenforum.de/posts/1512585/)

**Doors**
- Rough openings: width = Baurichtmaß + 10 mm, height = Baurichtmaß + 5 mm (e.g. 875 → 885 mm; 1000 × 2125 → 1010 × 2130 mm). — [letwork.de](https://letwork.de/ratgeber/tueren/normen-und-masse/rohbaumasse-tueren-und-fenster/); [heinze.de Novoferm sheet](https://heinze.de/m2/40/60840/pdf/85/22238885px595x842.pdf)
- Barrier-free (DIN 18040-2): apartment entrance door and bath/WC door **≥ 90 cm clear width**, interior doors **≥ 80 cm**; wheelchair-unrestricted use requires 90 cm. — [bau.de forum Behindertengerechtes Wohnen](https://bau.de/forum/behinder/10025.php); [TÜV SÜD FAQ Barrierefreiheit](https://www.tuvsud.com/de-de/indust-re/bautechnik-info/faq-uebersicht/faq-barrierefreiheit); [AGSV Bayern Faltblatt (PDF)](https://www.agsv.bayern.de/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/obb_faltblatt_barrierefr_wohnen.pdf)

**Window sills / parapets (BRH) and fall protection**
- Rule of thumb: up to **12 m fall height, 80 cm** parapet; above, **90 cm**; depends on the Land; Baden-Württemberg usually 80 cm if the parapet is ≥ 20 cm deep; if the sill is too low, a (sometimes higher) guard is required. — [heimwerker.de, Absturzsicherung Fenster](https://www.heimwerker.de/absturzsicherung-fenster/)
- A forum answer calls 90 cm the standard parapet height (citing DIN 18065 and LBOs) — conflicting/weak (AI-generated). — [bau.de forum 14156](https://bau.de/forum/neubau/14156.php)

**Radiators**
- **600 mm** panel radiator height is "the classic choice for living, bedroom and work rooms with standard window parapet" (Buderus listing). — [heizungsdiscount24.de, Buderus C-Profil 600 mm](https://www.heizungsdiscount24.de/heizkoerper/buderus-heizkoerper-flachheizkoerper-c-profil-typ-11-600x400-mm-h-x-l-kompakt.html)

### Inferences
- For a 3D model without a section drawing: default ceiling height 2,50 m (new build), 2,40–2,45 m (1950s–80s), 3,00–3,50 m (Gründerzeit Altbau); default BRH 0,80–0,90 m; radiator 0,60 m high under the window, plus a few cm clearance above floor and below sill (exact clearances not sourced).

## 4. Furniture placement guidance and clearances (DIN 18011, DIN 18022, DIN 18040-2, practical values)

### Takeaway
DIN 18011 ("Stellflächen, Abstände und Bewegungsflächen im Wohnungsbau", 1951/1967) and DIN 18022 (kitchens, baths, WCs, 1989) are **withdrawn** and only historical/recommendatory; the old norm logic was minimum movement depths of ~**70 cm** between furniture and wall. Today the reference for clearances is DIN 18040-2 (barrier-free: **120 × 120 cm** in front of furniture/sanitary/kitchen, **150 × 150 cm** for wheelchair "R" standard, corridors **≥ 120 cm**) plus practical values: **≥ 90 cm** in front of wardrobes with hinged doors, **60–70 cm** beside a bed, **100–120 cm** in front of kitchen counters, **80–100 cm** behind dining chairs, TV distance ≈ **1,2–1,6 × diagonal (4K)**.

### Cited Findings
**Status of the standards**
- DIN 18011 is listed by DIN as **withdrawn** ("Areas required for furniture etc., spacings and activity spaces in housing"). — [DIN NABau listing](https://www.din.de/en/getting-involved/standards-committees/nabau/publications/wdc-beuth:din21:1721435)

## 6. Common pitfalls when reading real-estate floor plans (exposé plans)

### Takeaway
Exposé plans are often redrawn, rescaled and marked "nicht maßstabsgetreu" / "ca."; they may mix finished and rough dimensions, use WoFlV-reduced m², and furniture symbols are illustrative. Always cross-check one printed dimension against the drawing, ask which area standard (WoFlV vs. DIN 277) was used, and verify critical walls (load-bearing?), ceiling heights and sloped zones with a section or on-site measurement (Aufmaß).

### Cited Findings
- Building plans usually show Rohbaumaße, exposé and as-built plans often Fertigmaße — rooms in reality are a few cm smaller than Rohbau dimensions. — [wunderloop.de](https://wunderloop.de/wissen/bibliothek/grundrisse/grundriss-lesen); [planprofi24.de](https://planprofi24.de/ratgeber/grundriss-richtig-lesen)
- Which dimension type a plan uses must be declared in legend/title block; there is no automatism (forum, architects' discussion). — [bau.de forum 12927](https://bau.de/forum/planung/12927.php); [bau.de forum 10789](https://bau.de/forum/architektur/10789.php)
- Symbols vary between offices/software; the plan legend governs. — [baufoerderung.de](https://baufoerderung.de/glossar/grundriss-lesen-symbole-verstehen-und-richtig-interpretieren/)
- Area figures depend on the standard: WoFlV vs. DIN 277 can differ massively (52 vs. 74 m² for the same attic flat). — [immobilienscout24.de, DIN 277](https://www.immobilienscout24.de/wissen/verkaufen/din277-instanz-fuer-wohn-und-nutzflaechen.html)
- "ca." areas are still tested against the BGH 10 % threshold in rental law; a stated area is a Beschaffenheitsvereinbarung. — [rewis.io, BGH VIII ZR 39/09](https://rewis.io/urteile/urteil/wcu-15-12-2009-viii-zr-3909/); [Berliner Mieterverein](https://www.berliner-mieterverein.de/recht/bgh/bgh0416.htm)
- Wall thickness on a plan does not reliably indicate load-bearing function (e.g. 11,5-cm walls marked either way); check the structural documentation before planning wall removals. — [bauexpertenforum, Tragende oder nicht tragende Wand?](https://www.bauexpertenforum.de/posts/1512585/); [bauexpertenforum, Tragende Wand? Statik fehlt](https://www.bauexpertenforum.de/threads/tragende-wand-statische-berechnung-fehlt-aktuell.143964/)
- Ceiling heights vary strongly by region and building; for a specific flat measure on site. — [mietpreise.info](https://www.mietpreise.info/site/deckenhoehe)

### Inferences
- Typical exposé traps (based on the cited conventions): (1) scale label "1:100" invalid after PDF resizing → calibrate on the longest printed dimension; (2) furniture symbols drawn undersized (e.g. 140-cm "double beds", 50-cm-deep wardrobes) to make rooms look larger → re-place with real sizes (Q5) and clearances (Q4); (3) door swings omitted or drawn into furniture; (4) radiators, shafts (Schächte), chimneys, pipe boxes (Vorwand-installation in bathrooms, typically 15–25 cm deep, unsourced) and window sills not shown, yet they block wall length; (5) sloped-ceiling flats: plan shows the floor outline, but furniture height is limited by the knee wall/slope — need a section; (6) balcony m² may be full or ¼/½-counted; (7) Altbau plans are often old and inaccurate (walls not square) → Aufmaß before ordering built-in furniture.
- Recommend for 3D planning: treat all exposé dimensions as ±2–5 cm, and mark "ca." values as uncertain inputs.

### Gaps
- No source found specifically on the legal weight of "nicht maßstabsgetreu" disclaimers in sale exposés, or on systematic studies of undersized furniture symbols in German real-estate plans.
- No source found on Verbraucherzentrale guidance specifically about reading Grundrisse (searches surfaced only general barrier-free flyers).
